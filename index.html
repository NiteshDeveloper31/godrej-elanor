The Philosophy
“Where French-inspired grace meets the tranquil murmur of Pune’s riverfront.”
8.5-Acre French Riverside Sanctuary

Godrej Elanor brings French-inspired architectural elegance and serene riverside living to Mahalunge, Pune. Spread across 8.5 acres, this RERA-registered development (PR1260002602617) by Godrej Properties features 5 residential towers rising above 3 podium levels, with 2 planned for the initial launch.

Embracing approximately 5 acres of open green sanctuary, lush riverside promenades meet timeless European design. Spacious 3 BHK residences feature expansive running decks, while exclusive 4 BHK duplex penthouses grace the uppermost four floors.
